# 🕉️ BhaktiAI – Devotional Companion

> **An AI-powered platform for exploring Indian spiritual, devotional, and cultural knowledge.**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-BhaktiAI-orange?style=for-the-badge)](https://yashwanth367.github.io/bhaktiai-devotional-companion/)

## 📌 Overview

**BhaktiAI – Devotional Companion** is an AI-powered digital platform designed to make Indian spiritual and cultural knowledge more accessible, interactive, and engaging.

The platform provides a modern way to explore **Indian scriptures, devotional traditions, temples, festivals, mantras, prayers, mythology, spiritual concepts, and cultural heritage** through an intuitive user experience.

BhaktiAI combines the accessibility of modern technology with India's rich spiritual and cultural heritage to create a digital companion for learning, exploration, and discovery.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🤖 **AI-Powered Devotional Companion**
  Interact with an intelligent assistant to explore spiritual and cultural topics.

* 📚 **Spiritual Knowledge Exploration**
  Discover concepts, teachings, traditions, and philosophies from Indian spiritual heritage.

* 📜 **Scriptures & Sacred Literature**
  Explore information related to important Indian scriptures and devotional texts.

* 🛕 **Temple & Heritage Information**
  Learn about temples, their historical significance, traditions, and cultural importance.

* 🪔 **Festivals & Traditions**
  Understand the history, significance, rituals, and cultural practices associated with Indian festivals.

* 📿 **Mantras & Prayers**
  Explore devotional mantras, prayers, meanings, and their cultural significance.

* 📖 **Stories & Mythology**
  Discover stories, legends, epics, and devotional narratives from Indian traditions.

* 🌺 **Cultural Heritage**
  Explore India's diverse traditions, customs, practices, and spiritual heritage.

* 💬 **Interactive User Experience**
  Access information through an intuitive and conversational interface.

## 🎯 Objectives

BhaktiAI aims to:

1. Make Indian spiritual and cultural knowledge easily accessible through technology.
2. Provide an interactive platform for learning and exploration.
3. Encourage younger generations to engage with India's cultural heritage.
4. Present complex spiritual concepts in an understandable format.
5. Create a digital gateway to India's diverse devotional traditions.
6. Promote awareness and appreciation of Indian cultural heritage.
